1

How to request deletion

In the Nimit Shop app: open the menu (≡), scroll to the bottom and tap "Request shop account deletion".
Or message the administrator on LINE at https://lin.ee/6rWI6rH or email aittinon417@gmail.com with the subject "Delete Nimit shop account".
Tell us the shop name and the phone number you sign in with, so we can verify that you own the shop.
2

Data that will be deleted

The shop's data, menus, photos and notification tokens.
Order data that identifies customers will be deleted or anonymized.
3

Data we may keep by law

Transaction and account records are kept for 5 years under accounting law, and system access logs as required by law, then deleted when the period ends.
4

Timeframe

We process the request within 30 days after your identity is verified.
